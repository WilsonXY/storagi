storagi

Four directions for Storagi

True black on T3 Code's theme, the koi as the one colour, ASCII where it carries meaning. Each prototype runs with sample files and a simulated host: drag, select, delete, upload and watch the speeds.

Variants

The locked screen, four ways

Every variant opens on the koi pond when you are signed out: the fish turn away from your cursor and fade where they pass under the sign-in.

Where it starts

  • 6 fails, 2 warnings from the taste inspector: the cream-and-terracotta assistant palette, four typefaces, 45 text blocks under 11px, 13 tiny uppercase labels, a gradient drop zone, a 45px landing-page title.
  • The Inbox starts below the fold at 1440×900; on a phone, more than a screen down.
  • Seven taglines on a working screen, and no speed, time left or download progress on transfers.
  • Manual refresh only, one file at a time, and delete through a modal with no undo.
